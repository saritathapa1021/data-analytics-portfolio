# U.S. Mental Health During COVID-19

**Tableau | Data Visualization | Public Health Analytics**

## Overview

This project analyzes anxiety and depression trends across the United States during and after the COVID-19 pandemic using CDC Household Pulse Survey data from 2020–2024.

The analysis was conducted in Tableau and includes national trends, state-level comparisons, demographic insights, and interactive visualizations.

- Tableau

- Data Visualization
- Public Health Analytics
- Trend Analysis
